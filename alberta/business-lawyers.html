About business law in Alberta

Business lawyers help with incorporation, shareholder and partnership agreements, commercial contracts, financing, and buying or selling a business. Larger firms also act on mergers and acquisitions.

A company can be incorporated provincially or federally, and which route is better depends on where you operate and your plans.

Business lawyers in Alberta: a short guide

What business lawyers help with

  • Starting a business and choosing a structure
  • Contracts, leases and terms of service
  • Buying or selling a business
  • Shareholder and partnership agreements
  • Financing, mergers and acquisitions
  • Trademarks and commercial agreements

How to choose a business lawyer

  1. Pick a firm that works with businesses of your size and in your industry.
  2. Ask whether you will deal with a partner or a more junior lawyer, and how that affects the fee.
  3. Check the rating and recent reviews for responsiveness, since deals run on deadlines.
  4. Confirm the lawyer is licensed with the provincial law society.

Questions to ask at your first call

  • What structure suits my business, and what are the trade-offs?
  • What should be in writing before I start working with a partner or investor?
  • What do you need from me to prepare the documents?
  • How do you charge for ongoing advice?
  • Which other advisers, such as an accountant, should be involved?

What to bring

  • A short description of the business and its owners
  • Existing contracts, leases or incorporation documents
  • Financial statements, if they exist
  • A description of the deal or problem and any deadline
  • Photo identification

How the process usually works

  1. You explain the goal and the key people involved.
  2. The lawyer proposes a structure and gives you a fee estimate.
  3. Documents are drafted, negotiated and revised.
  4. You sign and the lawyer files any required registrations.
  5. The firm stores the corporate records or hands them to you.
⚖

Check before you hire. You can confirm that a lawyer is licensed with the Law Society of Alberta. If cost is a worry, ask whether Legal Aid Alberta could help with your matter. LawDB is an independent directory: this page is general information, not legal advice.

Common questions

How do I choose a business lawyer in Alberta?

Pick a firm that works with businesses of your size and in your industry. Confirm the lawyer is licensed with the provincial law society.

What does a business lawyer cost in Alberta?

Incorporation is commonly a flat fee. Deals and negotiations are usually hourly or capped, so ask for an estimate with the main assumptions. Ask about disbursements (third-party costs), taxes, and what happens if your matter changes in scope. Many firms offer a first consultation, but confirm whether it is free.

What should I know about business law in Alberta?

A company can be incorporated provincially or federally, and which route is better depends on where you operate and your plans.
