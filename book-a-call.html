Frequently asked questions

What's actually in it for my firm?

A clear, personalized plan for how your firm shows up on LawDB, built around your practice areas and the cities you serve, instead of guessing your way through it alone. Most firms leave the call knowing exactly which pages they'll appear on and what it will take to start showing up for the searches that turn into real clients.

Will this actually help me bring in more clients?

That's the whole point of the call. LawDB puts your firm in front of people who have already told us what kind of case they have, whether that's a traffic ticket, a divorce, or a personal injury claim, so the traffic you get is already qualified. We'll walk through how firms use LawDB to turn that kind of intent-based traffic into actual retained clients.

I already rank on Google. Why would I need this?

Ranking on Google and being easy to find inside a directory built specifically around legal case types are two different things. LawDB catches searchers who are browsing directories rather than search engines, and firms that show up in both places consistently outperform firms that rely on just one channel. Browse the directory itself to see how firms are organized by case type and city.

How is this different from just filling out the sign-up form myself?

The listing form gets your firm on the map. The call is where we make sure it's set up to actually perform, the right practice-area tags, the right cities, and a clear read on whether Basic or Featured placement makes more sense for your growth goals. Ten minutes on the phone can save you months of a listing that's live but invisible.

Is 30 minutes really worth carving out of my day?

Compare it to the alternative: paying per click for search ads, or spending hours on your own website's SEO with no guarantee of results. This call costs you nothing and can put a free, working lead source in front of your firm by the end of the week. Most firms find it's the highest-value 30 minutes they spend that week.

What will I actually walk away with?

A firm answer on which plan fits your practice, a timeline for when your profile goes live, and a clear picture of what a fully optimized listing looks like for a firm like yours. If you decide to move forward with Featured placement, we can often get that set up before the call even ends.
