About immigration law in British Columbia

British Columbia runs its own provincial nominee program, the BC PNP, alongside federal immigration streams. Skills Immigration and the Entrepreneur Immigration stream operate on BC's own criteria and invitation rounds, and a provincial nomination is worth a substantial number of Express Entry points, which is often what makes an application viable.

Vancouver has one of the deepest immigration bars in the country, reflecting the province's role as a primary landing point, and several of the strongest-reviewed firms in this entire directory practise here. One practical note: make sure you are dealing with a lawyer or a licensed consultant (RCIC), and confirm which. Both are regulated, but they are different professions with different scope. Everyone listed below is a law firm. The firms below handle BC PNP and federal immigration matters.

Immigration lawyers in British Columbia: a short guide

What immigration lawyers help with

  • Visitor visas, study permits and work permits
  • Permanent residence applications
  • Family sponsorship
  • Refugee claims and appeals
  • Citizenship applications
  • Responding to refusals and procedural fairness letters

How to choose a immigration lawyer

  1. Make sure the person who will represent you is a licensed lawyer or another authorised representative, and ask for their licence details.
  2. Ask how often the firm handles your type of application.
  3. Read recent reviews for clear communication and honest timelines.
  4. Confirm the lawyer is licensed with the provincial law society.

Questions to ask at your first call

  • Which program fits my situation, and what are the alternatives?
  • What documents will I need, and how long do they take to get?
  • What is your fee, and what government fees are paid on top of it?
  • What can go wrong with my application?
  • How will you keep me updated?

What to bring

  • Passport and any travel documents
  • Your immigration history and any previous applications or refusals
  • Proof of education, work and language ability
  • Family details for you and anyone included
  • Any letters or notices from immigration authorities
⚖

Check before you hire. You can confirm that a lawyer is licensed with the Law Society of British Columbia. If cost is a worry, ask whether Legal Aid BC could help with your matter. LawDB is an independent directory: this page is general information, not legal advice.

Common questions

How do I choose a immigration lawyer in British Columbia?

Make sure the person who will represent you is a licensed lawyer or another authorised representative, and ask for their licence details. Confirm the lawyer is licensed with the provincial law society.

What does a immigration lawyer cost in British Columbia?

Immigration lawyers usually quote a fee for each application, plus government fees paid separately. Ask for a written quote and what is included if an application is refused.
