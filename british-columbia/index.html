Frequently asked questions

How many law firms does LawDB list in British Columbia?

LawDB lists 164 law firms across 10 cities in British Columbia.

How are lawyers regulated in British Columbia?

Lawyers in British Columbia are regulated by the Law Society of British Columbia. You can confirm a lawyer is licensed through its public directory.

What help is available in British Columbia if I cannot afford a lawyer?

You may qualify for help from Legal Aid BC. Many lawyers also offer a first consultation, so ask when you call.

Are Featured firms rated higher?

No. Ratings come from each firm's public Google listing and cannot be bought. Featured firms are partner placements shown at the top of the list.
