Traffic Ticket Lawyers in British Columbia

British Columbia · Traffic & Impaired Driving

A BC violation ticket is worth disputing when the penalty points threaten a driving prohibition, or when the ticket carries a Driver Risk Premium on top of the fine. You have 30 days to dispute, and simply paying the ticket is an admission that puts the points on your record.

The heavier end of BC driving law is where lawyers earn their fee: Immediate Roadside Prohibitions, excessive speeding with vehicle impoundment, and driving-while-prohibited charges. IRPs in particular run on a very short clock, seven days to apply for review, and are decided by RoadSafetyBC rather than a court, which is a different process with its own rules of evidence.
