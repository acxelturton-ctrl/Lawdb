A&M Canadian Immigration Law Corporation is a Winnipeg firm handling study permits, work permits, Express Entry, family sponsorships and citizenship matters. The firm was founded by Dr. Muhammad Abrar, a barrister and solicitor with a PhD in law from Glasgow University who began practising law in 2005.

Practice areas include study permit extensions, post-graduation work permits, LMIA work permits, visitor visas, the Super Visa, provincial nominations including all Manitoba Provincial Nominee Program streams, the Rural Community Immigration Program, spousal sponsorships and proof of citizenship. The firm also handles procedural fairness letters, refusals and reconsideration, appeals and Federal Court litigation, as well as U.S. immigration. It serves clients across Canada and internationally.

Before you call about an immigration application

General guidance for this kind of matter, not advice about your situation and not specific to this firm.

Questions worth asking

  • Are you a licensed lawyer, and will you be the one signing and submitting my application?
  • What are the realistic timelines and the main risks in my case?
  • Is your fee fixed for the whole application, and what costs such as government fees are extra?
  • What happens if my application is refused?

How this work is usually billed

Immigration lawyers commonly charge a flat fee per application or stage, plus government processing fees, which are paid separately. Ask for a written retainer that lists exactly what is covered.

What to have ready

Passport and status documents, proof of work and education history, language test results if you have them, and any prior applications or refusal letters. Manitoba runs its own Provincial Nominee Program alongside federal streams, so tell your lawyer if you have a Manitoba job offer or connections. Only lawyers in good standing with the Law Society of Manitoba and licensed immigration consultants may represent you for a fee.

You can confirm that any lawyer is licensed through the Law Society of Manitoba.
