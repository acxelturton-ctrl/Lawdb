Keystone Law Group is a Surrey firm of lawyers and notaries handling business, real estate, immigration, and wills and estates matters.

The firm's business practice includes commercial loan transactions, business purchases and sales, incorporations, societies and business registrations, corporate record preparation, and drafting of contracts and leases, and it also handles property development. It answers questions on probate fees in British Columbia, corporate record keeping, and the wills variation provisions of the Wills, Estates and Succession Act. Keystone emphasizes timely responses and offers home visits, and many clients come through referrals from family, friends and business colleagues.

Before you call about business / corporate

General guidance for this kind of matter, not advice about your situation and not specific to this firm.

Questions worth asking

  • Have you worked with businesses at my stage and size?
  • Flat fee or hourly for this piece of work?
  • What will you need from me to keep the cost down?
  • Who handles it if this later turns into a dispute?

How this work is usually billed

Defined tasks, incorporation, a shareholder agreement, a lease review, are commonly flat-fee. Ongoing or transactional work is hourly. Some firms offer a monthly arrangement for steady advisory work.

What to have ready

Your incorporation documents if you have them, any agreement you are being asked to sign, and a plain description of what you are trying to achieve.
