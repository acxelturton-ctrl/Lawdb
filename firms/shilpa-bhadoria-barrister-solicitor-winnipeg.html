Before you call about a property or estate matter

General guidance for this kind of matter, not advice about your situation and not specific to this firm.

Questions worth asking

  • Is your fee fixed, and which disbursements such as title searches and registration costs are extra?
  • Who will actually handle my file, and how can I reach them?
  • What are the key dates, and what happens if one is missed?
  • Can you review the agreement before I sign it?

How this work is usually billed

Residential real estate work is commonly quoted as a flat fee plus disbursements, while wills and powers of attorney are often a fixed price per document set. Ask for the total in writing, including taxes and registration costs.

What to have ready

Your agreement of purchase and sale, ID, mortgage details, and any condo or survey documents. For a will, a list of your assets, the people you want to name as executor and guardians, and who should hold your power of attorney. Manitoba charges a provincial land transfer tax on most property purchases, so ask your lawyer to include it in the closing costs.

You can confirm that any lawyer is licensed through the Law Society of Manitoba.
