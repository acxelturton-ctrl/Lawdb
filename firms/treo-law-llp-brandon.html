Before you call about a legal matter

General guidance for this kind of matter, not advice about your situation and not specific to this firm.

Questions worth asking

  • Have you handled a matter like mine, and what happened?
  • Who will do the day-to-day work, and at what rates?
  • What is a realistic range for total cost, and when will you tell me if it is going to change?
  • Is there a way to resolve this without going to court?

How this work is usually billed

Business and litigation work is usually billed hourly, with rates that vary by lawyer seniority. Ask for an estimate range, how often you will be billed, and whether a retainer is needed.

What to have ready

The contracts, emails and letters connected to the issue, a short timeline of events, and the names of everyone involved. In Manitoba, civil claims above the small claims limit are heard in the Court of King’s Bench, and many disputes are resolved through negotiation or mediation first.

You can confirm that any lawyer is licensed through the Law Society of Manitoba.
