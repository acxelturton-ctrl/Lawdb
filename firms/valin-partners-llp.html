Before you call about employment dispute

General guidance for this kind of matter, not advice about your situation and not specific to this firm.

Questions worth asking

  • Is the severance I have been offered reasonable for my age, role, and years of service?
  • What is the deadline I need to worry about?
  • Do you offer a flat-fee review before I commit to anything larger?
  • What is the realistic best and worst outcome if we push back?

How this work is usually billed

Many employment lawyers offer a fixed-fee severance review, which is a cheap way to find out whether an offer is low. Negotiation or litigation beyond that is hourly, sometimes partly contingency.

What to have ready

Your employment contract, the termination letter and severance offer, recent pay statements, and your start date.
