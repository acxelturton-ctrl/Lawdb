Before you call about buying or selling a home

General guidance for this kind of matter, not advice about your situation and not specific to this firm.

Questions worth asking

  • Is your fee flat, and does it include title insurance, disbursements and registration costs?
  • Can you review the condominium documents, and would you flag a special assessment or reserve fund problem before I firm up the deal?
  • Is your closing fee a flat fee, and what is included in it?
  • Who will review the purchase contract and condominium documents before my deadline?

How this work is usually billed

Residential closings are commonly a flat fee plus disbursements such as title searches, registration fees and, if you buy it, title insurance. Alberta does not charge a land transfer tax, but registration fees at Land Titles depend on the property value and mortgage amount.

What to have ready

The signed purchase contract, your mortgage commitment, condominium documents if applicable, and government-issued ID. If you are buying a condominium, ask the seller for the condominium documents as early as possible so the lawyer can review them.
