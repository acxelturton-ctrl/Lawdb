Why list with us

We are a young directory and we would rather tell you exactly what a listing does and does not do than sell you a number we cannot back up.

Found by case type, not just name

Most people looking for a lawyer do not know a firm's name — they know they have a custody dispute, an ICBC claim, or a closing next month. Our search starts from the case type, then narrows by region, so you are matched on the work you actually do.

A real page, not a phone-book row

Every firm gets its own profile page with your practice areas, location, map, contact details, and a link to your own website. It is a page we maintain and keep indexed, not a row buried in a list.

Practice-area pages per province

We publish dedicated pages for each practice area in each province we cover, and firms tagged for that area appear on them. That is additional surface area pointing at your firm beyond your own profile.

We check things before publishing

Listings are built from public business data and verified by hand as we go — we only link out to a firm website once we have confirmed it belongs to that firm. If something on your listing is wrong, tell us and we will fix it.

Get Featured

Featured changes where and how your firm is shown on LawDB. It does not guarantee calls, clients or search-engine rankings, and LawDB is a new directory, so we do not quote traffic or lead numbers. How it works

  1. Create a free account. Sign in with Google or an email link. No password, and it takes under a minute.
  2. Add or claim your firm. Claim your existing listing or add a new one: contact details, practice areas and city.
  3. We check it, then it goes live. We confirm the details so members can trust what they see. Featured upgrades go live as soon as the subscription is confirmed.
  4. Run it yourself. Update your profile any time from your dashboard, with no emailing back and forth. Members can follow and rate your firm.

Being straight with you

LawDB launched recently. We are not going to quote you traffic figures, lead counts, or search rankings, because we do not have a meaningful track record to quote yet — and any directory that does quote you those numbers this early is guessing.

What we can tell you is what exists: 1,092 firms across Ontario, Quebec, British Columbia, Alberta and Manitoba, 62 cities, and 10 practice areas, each with its own indexed page, a sitemap submitted to search engines, and original written content published on the site regularly.

A free listing costs you nothing and gives you a linked, indexed page. Featured is worth considering if you want priority placement in a competitive city while the directory is still small — which is exactly when placement is easiest to get.
