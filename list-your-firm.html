Frequently asked questions

Is the Basic listing actually free, or is there a catch?

It's genuinely free. No credit card, no trial period that quietly turns into a charge. You get a marker on the map, a profile page listing your services, and placement by case type and city. Most directories charge just to get listed at all, so this is a real chance to build visibility with zero downside.

What's the actual difference between Basic and Featured?

Basic gets you found. Featured gets you found first. A Featured listing puts your firm at the top of results for your practice areas, adds a highlighted marker on the map, puts you first in the live search dropdown, shows your firm in the homepage Featured Partners showcase, and gives you an expanded profile with a cover image, practice-area tags and a consultation request form. Featured improves where and how your firm is shown on LawDB; it does not guarantee calls, clients or search-engine rankings. You can see the full comparison on our for firms page.

How long until my profile is actually live?

Once you submit the form, our team reviews your details and aims to upload your listing within 24 hours. Featured listings go live once your payment is confirmed. We check what you send us so your profile is accurate from day one, rather than publishing something you'll have to come back and fix later.

Is there a contract if I choose Featured?

No. Featured is a straightforward monthly subscription and you can cancel any time. We'd rather earn your business every month than lock you into something you're not seeing results from.

Can I update my listing later, like adding new practice areas or a new location?

Yes. Just reach out to us at info@lawdb.ca with what's changed and we'll update your profile. There's no fee for edits to an existing listing.

What does LawDB actually do that Google doesn't already do for me?

Google shows searchers a wall of options with no context. LawDB organizes firms by the actual problem someone has, like a traffic ticket, a divorce, or a personal injury claim, so people who find you are already looking for the kind of help you offer. You can browse how listings are structured on our find-your-lawyer page.

I'd rather talk it through before submitting anything. Is that an option?

Definitely. You can book a 30-minute call with our team and we'll walk you through the whole process, answer your questions, and help you decide which plan makes sense for your firm. No pressure, no obligation to sign up afterward.
