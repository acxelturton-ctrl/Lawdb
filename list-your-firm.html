How listing works

Four steps, all in your account.

  1. Create a free accountContinue with Google, or get a one-time sign-in link by email. There is no password to remember.
  2. Add or claim your firmSearch the directory. If your firm is listed, ask to manage it. If not, add it with your address, phone, hours and practice areas.
  3. We check, then you are liveWe confirm you are connected to the firm, then publish. Your map pin is placed from your address, and you get an email when you are approved.
  4. Run your listing yourselfEdit details any time, add a logo, cover and photos, read enquiries, and see your followers and ratings. Upgrade to Featured whenever you like.

Frequently asked questions

Is the Basic listing really free?

Yes. No credit card and no trial that turns into a charge. You get your own profile page, a pin on the map, placement by case type and city, and an enquiries inbox. You can keep a Basic listing for as long as you like.

Do I have to email anyone to get listed?

No. Everything happens in your account. Create a free account with Google or an email link, then search for your firm. If it is already listed, ask to manage it. If it is not, add it yourself. We review each request, and you get an email when it is approved.

How do you check that I am allowed to manage a firm?

We review every request by hand. For example, we compare your email address with the firm's website, and we may ask a follow-up question. This keeps listings trustworthy for the people searching.

How long until my profile is live?

Most requests are reviewed within a day. A new listing goes public when it is approved, and the map pin is placed automatically from your address (or the middle of your city if the address cannot be found).

What is the difference between Basic and Featured?

Basic gets you found. Featured gets you found first: your firm is placed above Basic firms for your city and case type, has a gold Featured pin and badge, appears in the homepage showcase, and your articles on LawDB News carry a Featured badge and get their own story pages that search engines can find. Featured is $89 a month.

Is there a contract if I choose Featured?

No. Featured is a monthly subscription. You can update your card or cancel yourself from your dashboard. We would rather earn your business every month than lock you in.

Can I update my listing later?

Yes, any time and at no cost. Sign in, open My firms, and edit your details, hours, practice areas, logo, cover and photos. There is no need to email us.

What can other people do with my listing?

Signed-in members can follow your firm and rate it. Firms only see the number of followers, never who they are. Star ratings count at once; a written comment is shown only after we review it, and never with the member's name.

I would rather talk it through first. Is that an option?

Yes. Book a short call and we will walk you through it and answer your questions, with no pitch.

Ready to get listed?

It takes a few minutes and costs nothing. Prefer to talk it through first? Book a short call.
