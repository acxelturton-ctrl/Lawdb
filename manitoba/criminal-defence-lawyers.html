About criminal defence law in Manitoba

Criminal defence lawyers represent people charged with offences, from impaired driving and assault to fraud and drug charges, at every stage from the first police contact to trial or appeal.

If cost is a concern, you may qualify for help from Legal Aid Manitoba. A defence lawyer can also advise you whether it makes sense to apply.

Criminal Defence lawyers in Manitoba: a short guide

What criminal defence lawyers help with

  • Bail hearings and release conditions
  • Assault, theft, fraud and drug charges
  • Impaired driving and other driving charges
  • Charges involving weapons or domestic incidents
  • Youth justice matters
  • Appeals and record suspensions

How to choose a criminal defence lawyer

  1. Look for a firm that defends criminal charges as its main work and appears regularly in the court where your case will be heard.
  2. Ask how quickly the firm can act, since early steps such as bail can matter.
  3. Check the rating and recent reviews for how clients were kept informed.
  4. Confirm the lawyer is licensed with the provincial law society.

Questions to ask at your first call

  • What are the possible outcomes, and what do you recommend?
  • What should I do and not do while this is going on?
  • How do you charge, and what is the retainer?
  • Who will appear in court for me?
  • What does this mean for my job, travel or immigration status?

What to bring

  • Any charge documents, court dates and release papers
  • Disclosure from the Crown, if you have it
  • A written account of what happened, kept private between you and your lawyer
  • Names of witnesses
  • Photo identification

How the process usually works

  1. The lawyer gets the charges and the disclosure from the Crown.
  2. They advise you on bail, evidence and options.
  3. Pre-trial discussions with the Crown may lead to a resolution.
  4. If there is no resolution, the case goes to trial.
  5. After a verdict there may be sentencing or an appeal.

Common questions

How do I choose a criminal defence lawyer in Manitoba?

Look for a firm that defends criminal charges as its main work and appears regularly in the court where your case will be heard. Confirm the lawyer is licensed with the provincial law society.

What does a criminal defence lawyer cost in Manitoba?

Criminal defence is often quoted as a flat fee per stage, or a retainer billed hourly. If cost is a concern, ask whether you may qualify for legal aid. Ask about disbursements (third-party costs), taxes, and what happens if your matter changes in scope. Many firms offer a first consultation, but confirm whether it is free.
