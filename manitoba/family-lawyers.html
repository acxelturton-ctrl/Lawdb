About family law in Manitoba

Family lawyers advise on separation and divorce, parenting arrangements, child and spousal support, and the division of property. Many also prepare separation agreements and marriage contracts.

Manitoba's Limitation of Actions Act sets a basic two-year limitation period for most claims, with exceptions. Speak to a lawyer early so that a deadline does not run out.

Divorce is governed by the federal Divorce Act, while property division and some other matters are decided under provincial law, so both matter in most separations.

Family lawyers in Manitoba: a short guide

What family law lawyers help with

  • Separation, divorce and separation agreements
  • Parenting time, decision-making and child support
  • Spousal support
  • Dividing property and debts
  • Marriage contracts and cohabitation agreements
  • Changing an existing order or agreement

How to choose a family law lawyer

  1. Look for a firm that does family law as a main part of its practice, not a side service.
  2. Ask whether the firm also offers mediation or collaborative approaches if you want to avoid court.
  3. Read recent reviews for how clients were treated at a stressful time.
  4. Confirm the lawyer is licensed with the provincial law society.

Questions to ask at your first call

  • What are my options, and what do you recommend for my situation?
  • How is the fee set, and is there a retainer?
  • What are the steps from here, and how long do they usually take?
  • What documents will I need to disclose?
  • How can I keep costs down?

What to bring

  • Marriage or relationship details and the date of separation
  • Information about your children and current arrangements
  • Income documents, such as tax returns and pay stubs
  • A list of assets, debts and accounts
  • Any agreements, court papers or letters

How the process usually works

  1. You explain your situation and your goals.
  2. The lawyer advises on rights and options and sets out a plan.
  3. Financial and other information is exchanged.
  4. Settlement is explored through negotiation or mediation.
  5. If needed, the matter proceeds to court for a decision.

Common questions

How do I choose a family law lawyer in Manitoba?

Look for a firm that does family law as a main part of its practice, not a side service. Confirm the lawyer is licensed with the provincial law society.

What does a family law lawyer cost in Manitoba?

Family lawyers usually require an upfront retainer and bill hourly. Ask how fees change if the case settles early, and whether limited-scope help is offered. Ask about disbursements (third-party costs), taxes, and what happens if your matter changes in scope. Many firms offer a first consultation, but confirm whether it is free.

What should I know about family law law in Manitoba?
