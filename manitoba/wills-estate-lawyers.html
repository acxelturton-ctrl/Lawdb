About wills and estates law in Manitoba

Wills and estates lawyers prepare wills and powers of attorney, help families administer an estate after a death, and advise on estate disputes. Many firms in this area also handle real estate, so the two often appear together.

In Manitoba, planning for incapacity usually means an enduring power of attorney and a health care directive. Ask your lawyer which documents fit your situation.

Wills & Estates lawyers in Manitoba: a short guide

What wills and estates lawyers help with

  • Wills and powers of attorney
  • Estate planning, including trusts
  • Applying to administer an estate after a death
  • Estate disputes and contested wills
  • Guardianship and capacity questions
  • Updating documents after a marriage, separation or birth

How to choose a wills and estates lawyer

  1. Decide whether you need documents drawn up, or help with an estate after someone has died, since firms often lean one way.
  2. Ask how the firm handles a family with property in more than one province or country.
  3. Read recent client reviews for patience and clear explanations, because these matters are personal.
  4. Confirm the lawyer is licensed with the provincial law society.

Questions to ask at your first call

  • Which documents do I need, and why?
  • What happens to my estate if I do not have a will?
  • How should I choose who acts for me, and what does that role involve?
  • How often should these documents be reviewed?
  • Where should the originals be kept?

What to bring

  • A list of your assets, debts and insurance
  • The names and contact details of the people you want to name
  • Any existing wills or powers of attorney
  • Photo identification
  • For an estate: the death certificate and the will, if there is one

How the process usually works

  1. You describe your family, assets and wishes in a first meeting.
  2. The lawyer drafts the documents and reviews them with you.
  3. You sign with the required witnesses, usually at the lawyer's office.
  4. The originals are stored safely and you are told where they are.
  5. For an estate, the lawyer prepares the court application, notifies beneficiaries and distributes the estate.

Common questions

How do I choose a wills and estates lawyer in Manitoba?

Decide whether you need documents drawn up, or help with an estate after someone has died, since firms often lean one way. Confirm the lawyer is licensed with the provincial law society.

What does a wills and estates lawyer cost in Manitoba?

Simple wills and powers of attorney are often quoted as a flat fee. Estate administration is more variable, so ask whether the quote is flat, hourly or a percentage. Ask about disbursements (third-party costs), taxes, and what happens if your matter changes in scope. Many firms offer a first consultation, but confirm whether it is free.

What should I know about wills and estates law in Manitoba?

In Manitoba, planning for incapacity usually means an enduring power of attorney and a health care directive. Ask your lawyer which documents fit your situation.
