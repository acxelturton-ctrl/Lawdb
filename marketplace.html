Questions

Are there any inquiries to unlock right now?

No. Nothing is available right now. Inquiries will come soon, and we have not set a date.

Who will be able to use the Lead Marketplace?

Law firms that LawDB has verified. At launch, verification is manual: we check your law society registration.

Will it cost anything to join?

No. Joining and browsing will be free for every firm, with or without a Featured listing. You pay only when you unlock an inquiry, with prepaid credits.

What will a firm see before it unlocks an inquiry?

The practice area, the city and province, how urgent it is, how long ago it came in, and a one-line summary that does not identify the person. Name, email and phone stay hidden until you unlock it.

Can two firms get the same inquiry?

No. The first firm to unlock an inquiry receives it, and it then disappears for every other firm.

What if an inquiry turns out to be junk?

You will be able to report an inquiry within 48 hours of unlocking it. If it is clearly junk, such as a disconnected number or spam, the credits go back to your balance after we review the report. A refund never depends on whether the person hires you.

Is this a referral service?

No. LawDB is an independent directory, not a law firm, and does not recommend any firm. The price of an inquiry is a flat advertising fee set by LawDB. It does not depend on whether the person hires you, and LawDB takes no share of any fee. Each firm remains responsible for following its own law society's rules on advertising and soliciting.

The Lead Marketplace is separate from Featured and does not need it.
