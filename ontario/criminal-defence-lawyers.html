About criminal defence law in Ontario

Being charged with a criminal offence in Ontario starts a process with real deadlines and real stakes, whether the charge is assault, theft, fraud, drug possession, or something more serious. What happens in the first few weeks - whether you retain a lawyer before your first court date, how a bail hearing is handled, what disclosure is requested - often shapes how the rest of the case goes.

Criminal defence lawyers in Ontario typically practice in the specific region where they appear regularly, since they know the local Crown attorneys and how a particular courthouse tends to run. The firms listed here handle criminal matters across Ontario; a few have particularly strong track records in specific types of charges, which you can see reflected in their reviews and profile pages.

Criminal Defence lawyers in Ontario: a short guide

What criminal defence lawyers help with

  • Bail hearings and release conditions
  • Assault, theft, fraud and drug charges
  • Impaired driving and other driving charges
  • Charges involving weapons or domestic incidents
  • Youth justice matters
  • Appeals and record suspensions

How to choose a criminal defence lawyer

  1. Look for a firm that defends criminal charges as its main work and appears regularly in the court where your case will be heard.
  2. Ask how quickly the firm can act, since early steps such as bail can matter.
  3. Check the rating and recent reviews for how clients were kept informed.
  4. Confirm the lawyer is licensed with the provincial law society.

Questions to ask at your first call

  • What are the possible outcomes, and what do you recommend?
  • What should I do and not do while this is going on?
  • How do you charge, and what is the retainer?
  • Who will appear in court for me?
  • What does this mean for my job, travel or immigration status?

What to bring

  • Any charge documents, court dates and release papers
  • Disclosure from the Crown, if you have it
  • A written account of what happened, kept private between you and your lawyer
  • Names of witnesses
  • Photo identification
