Frequently asked questions

How many law firms does LawDB list in Ontario?

LawDB lists 571 law firms across 30 cities in Ontario.

How are lawyers regulated in Ontario?

Lawyers in Ontario are regulated by the Law Society of Ontario. You can confirm a lawyer is licensed through its public directory.

What help is available in Ontario if I cannot afford a lawyer?

You may qualify for help from Legal Aid Ontario. Many lawyers also offer a first consultation, so ask when you call.

Are Featured firms rated higher?

No. Ratings come from each firm's public Google listing and cannot be bought. Featured firms are partner placements shown at the top of the list.
