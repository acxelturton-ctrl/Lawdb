How to choose a lawyer in Kitchener

  1. Start with the kind of case you have, then narrow by practice area.
  2. Check that the lawyer is licensed with the Law Society of Ontario.
  3. Compare Google ratings together with the number of reviews.
  4. Ask about fees, retainers and billing before you sign.
  5. If the matter is large, speak to more than one firm.

Frequently asked questions

How many law firms does LawDB list in Kitchener?

LawDB lists 11 law firms in Kitchener.

How do I find a good lawyer in Kitchener?

Start with the kind of case you have, then compare firms by Google rating, number of reviews and location. Confirm the lawyer is licensed with the Law Society of Ontario and ask how fees are charged before you sign.

Are Featured firms rated higher?

No. Ratings come from each firm's public Google listing. Featured firms are partner placements shown above the other listings, so read the rating and reviews as well as the badge.

Where can I get legal help in Kitchener if I cannot afford a lawyer?

You may qualify for help from Legal Aid Ontario. Many lawyers also offer a first consultation, and some offer limited-scope services, so ask when you call.

Do Kitchener lawyers handle cases outside the city?

Many do. Courts and clients are often spread across the region, so firms in Kitchener commonly act for people in nearby communities as well. Check the firm's profile or ask when you call.
