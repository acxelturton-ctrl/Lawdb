About business law in Quebec

Business law in Quebec spans the same range as elsewhere, from a first incorporation through to complex commercial transactions, but with a few provincial specifics worth knowing. Companies can incorporate provincially under the Quebec Business Corporations Act or federally, and the choice has real consequences for registration and reporting. Registration with the Registraire des entreprises is required either way.

Quebec also has language obligations that businesses elsewhere do not: the Charter of the French Language governs contracts, workplace communications, commercial signage, and, since recent amendments, trademarks and product labelling. Getting that wrong is a common and avoidable problem for companies expanding into the province. Business lawyers in Quebec: a short guide

What business lawyers help with

  • Starting a business and choosing a structure
  • Contracts, leases and terms of service
  • Buying or selling a business
  • Shareholder and partnership agreements
  • Financing, mergers and acquisitions
  • Trademarks and commercial agreements

How to choose a business lawyer

  1. Pick a firm that works with businesses of your size and in your industry.
  2. Ask whether you will deal with a partner or a more junior lawyer, and how that affects the fee.
  3. Check the rating and recent reviews for responsiveness, since deals run on deadlines.
  4. Confirm the lawyer is licensed with the provincial law society.

Questions to ask at your first call

  • What structure suits my business, and what are the trade-offs?
  • What should be in writing before I start working with a partner or investor?
  • What do you need from me to prepare the documents?
  • How do you charge for ongoing advice?
  • Which other advisers, such as an accountant, should be involved?

What to bring

  • A short description of the business and its owners
  • Existing contracts, leases or incorporation documents
  • Financial statements, if they exist
  • A description of the deal or problem and any deadline
  • Photo identification
⚖

Check before you hire. You can confirm that a lawyer is licensed with the Barreau du Québec. If cost is a worry, ask whether the Commission des services juridiques (legal aid) could help with your matter. LawDB is an independent directory: this page is general information, not legal advice.

Common questions

How do I choose a business lawyer in Quebec?

Pick a firm that works with businesses of your size and in your industry. Confirm the lawyer is licensed with the provincial law society.

What does a business lawyer cost in Quebec?

Business lawyers may quote a fixed fee for a defined task, such as an incorporation, and bill by time for open-ended work. Ask which applies to your matter.
