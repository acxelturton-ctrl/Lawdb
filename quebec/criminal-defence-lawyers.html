About criminal defence law in Quebec

Criminal law is federal, so the Criminal Code applies the same way in Quebec as everywhere else in Canada. What differs is the local machinery: charges are prosecuted by the Directeur des poursuites criminelles et penales (DPCP), and cases move through the Cour du Quebec at the palais de justice serving your region.

The early stage matters more than most people expect. Whether you have a lawyer before your first appearance, how a bail hearing is handled, and how quickly disclosure is requested all shape what options remain later. Quebec criminal lawyers tend to practice within the region where they appear regularly, because knowing the local prosecutors and how a particular courthouse runs is a real advantage. Criminal Defence lawyers in Quebec: a short guide

What criminal defence lawyers help with

  • Bail hearings and release conditions
  • Assault, theft, fraud and drug charges
  • Impaired driving and other driving charges
  • Charges involving weapons or domestic incidents
  • Youth justice matters
  • Appeals and record suspensions

How to choose a criminal defence lawyer

  1. Look for a firm that defends criminal charges as its main work and appears regularly in the court where your case will be heard.
  2. Ask how quickly the firm can act, since early steps such as bail can matter.
  3. Check the rating and recent reviews for how clients were kept informed.
  4. Confirm the lawyer is licensed with the provincial law society.

Questions to ask at your first call

  • What are the possible outcomes, and what do you recommend?
  • What should I do and not do while this is going on?
  • How do you charge, and what is the retainer?
  • Who will appear in court for me?
  • What does this mean for my job, travel or immigration status?

What to bring

  • Any charge documents, court dates and release papers
  • Disclosure from the Crown, if you have it
  • A written account of what happened, kept private between you and your lawyer
  • Names of witnesses
  • Photo identification
⚖

Check before you hire. You can confirm that a lawyer is licensed with the Barreau du Québec. If cost is a worry, ask whether the Commission des services juridiques (legal aid) could help with your matter. LawDB is an independent directory: this page is general information, not legal advice.

Look for a firm that defends criminal charges as its main work and appears regularly in the court where your case will be heard. Confirm the lawyer is licensed with the provincial law society.

What does a criminal defence lawyer cost in Quebec?

Criminal defence fees depend on the charge and how far the case goes. Ask for a written fee agreement that says what stage of the case it covers.
