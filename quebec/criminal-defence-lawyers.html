Criminal Defence Lawyers in Quebec

Quebec · Criminal Defence

Criminal law is federal, so the Criminal Code applies the same way in Quebec as everywhere else in Canada. What differs is the local machinery: charges are prosecuted by the Directeur des poursuites criminelles et penales (DPCP), and cases move through the Cour du Quebec at the palais de justice serving your region.

The early stage matters more than most people expect. Whether you have a lawyer before your first appearance, how a bail hearing is handled, and how quickly disclosure is requested all shape what options remain later. Quebec criminal lawyers tend to practice within the region where they appear regularly, because knowing the local prosecutors and how a particular courthouse runs is a real advantage. The firms below handle criminal matters across the province.
