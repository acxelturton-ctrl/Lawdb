Frequently asked questions

How many law firms does LawDB list in Quebec?

LawDB lists 167 law firms across 18 cities in Quebec.

How are lawyers regulated in Quebec?

Lawyers in Quebec are regulated by the Barreau du Québec. You can confirm a lawyer is licensed through its public directory.

What help is available in Quebec if I cannot afford a lawyer?

You may qualify for help from the Commission des services juridiques (legal aid). Many lawyers also offer a first consultation, so ask when you call.

Are Featured firms rated higher?

No. Ratings come from each firm's public Google listing and cannot be bought. Featured firms are partner placements shown at the top of the list.
