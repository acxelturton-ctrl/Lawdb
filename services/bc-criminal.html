Criminal Defence Lawyers in British Columbia

British Columbia · Criminal Defence

The Criminal Code is federal and applies the same across Canada, but BC runs its charge-approval process differently from most provinces. Here, Crown counsel screens charges before they are laid, applying a substantial likelihood of conviction standard. That means a strong defence submission made early, before charges are approved, can sometimes stop a prosecution from starting at all. It is a genuine opportunity, and it closes quickly.

Cases proceed through Provincial Court or BC Supreme Court depending on the offence and elections made. Lawyers tend to practise within the region where they appear regularly, since familiarity with local Crown counsel matters. The firms below handle criminal matters across British Columbia.
