Traffic Ticket Lawyers in Canada

A traffic ticket lawyer helps people who have been given a ticket or summons for a provincial offence such as speeding, careless driving, stunt driving or running a red light. They can explain the penalties, appear for you and challenge the ticket where there is a defence. Because a conviction can add demerit points and raise insurance costs, many drivers ask for advice before they simply pay.

When to speak to a traffic ticket lawyer

  • You received a ticket and are not sure whether to pay it or dispute it.
  • The offence is serious, such as stunt driving or careless driving, or could put your licence at risk.
  • You are worried about demerit points or higher insurance premiums.
  • You drive for work and a licence suspension would affect your job.
  • You missed a deadline to respond or a court date.

What a traffic ticket lawyer does

  • Explains your options on the ticket, such as paying it, disputing it or asking to resolve it with the prosecutor.
  • Reviews the evidence, including how the offence was measured or observed.
  • May be able to appear in court on your behalf, so that you do not have to attend.
  • Negotiates a lesser charge where that is appropriate.
  • Advises on licence suspensions and on how a conviction could affect your insurance.

How the rules differ across Canada

Traffic offences are mostly provincial, so the penalties, demerit point systems, deadlines and the process for disputing a ticket all differ by province. The ticket itself names the court and explains how to respond. Act quickly: an unanswered ticket can lead to a conviction by default and further penalties.

Questions to ask before you hire a traffic ticket lawyer

  1. Have you handled this type of ticket in this court before?
  2. What are the realistic outcomes, and what will it cost compared with simply paying the fine?
  3. Do I need to attend in person?
  4. How will a conviction, or a reduced charge, affect my licence and insurance?

Frequently asked questions

Should I pay my ticket or fight it?

Paying usually counts as a guilty plea, which can bring demerit points and a possible insurance increase. Disputing the ticket keeps your options open. A lawyer can tell you whether it is worth contesting in your situation.

What happens if I ignore a traffic ticket?

In most provinces an unanswered ticket results in a conviction by default and can lead to a licence suspension or further penalties. Check the response instructions on your ticket, and get advice before any deadline passes.

Are stunt driving and careless driving serious tickets?

They are more serious than a standard speeding ticket and can carry heavier penalties, including licence suspensions in some provinces. If you are charged with one, speak to a lawyer quickly.
